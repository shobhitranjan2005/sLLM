Our final model uses a lossless SentencePiece Unigram tokenizer (vocab_size=8192, byte-fallback, identity normalization, no dummy prefix) instead of raw bytes, since Devanagari costs roughly 3x a byte tokenizer's sequence length for no semantic gain. The architecture replaces learned absolute position embeddings with RoPE, LayerNorm with RMSNorm, and the GELU MLP with SwiGLU (hidden dim shrunk ~2/3 to hold parameter count roughly constant), plus GPT-2-style scaled residual init on each block's output projection, for 1,827,968 total parameters against the 2,000,000 cap. We trained with plain Adam (lr=3e-3, 100-step linear warmup, cosine decay to 10% of peak, global gradient-norm clipping at 1.0) rather than the Muon+AdamW split we also tried: Muon lost an ablation by 0.83% relative bpb (1.7210 vs. 1.7067) at roughly equal wall time, most likely because we didn't independently tune each optimizer's learning rate and because Muon's usual per-step advantage is more established at larger batch sizes than our batch of 8. We are shipping the simpler, better-scoring plain-Adam run rather than the more novel optimizer, in line with the assignment's own preference for changes that demonstrably help over complexity for its own sake. Final dev bpb = 1.7067, a 28.0% relative improvement over the byte-tokenizer baseline (2.3718) reported in the project's pre-existing reference numbers.
